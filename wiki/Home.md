## About Pexos

Pexos was created by **Wadim Seminsky** and **Bertin van Vliet** as a vibrant creative platform where artists can unleash their imaginations and bring drawings to life. It's a celebration of art, innovation, and community.

The platform is built with **SvelteKit**, **Supabase**, **Three.js**, and **Tailwind CSS** / **DaisyUI**, and is deployed on **Netlify** and **Vercel**.
